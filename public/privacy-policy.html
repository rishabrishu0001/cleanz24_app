Privacy Policy for Cleanz24

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Welcome to Cleanz24 - Premium Laundry & Dry Cleaning ("Cleanz24", "we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring your personal information is handled safely and responsibly.

1. Information We Collect

To provide our laundry, dry cleaning, and doorstep pickup and delivery services, we collect:

  • Personal Information: Mobile phone number (for secure OTP login & delivery updates), Full Name, and optional Email.
  • Delivery Addresses: Pickup & drop-off addresses provided by you (Home, Office, or Other).
  • Approximate Location: Requested only with your explicit permission to determine the nearest studio and delivery estimates.
  • Order Details: Garment service choices and active order history.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Authenticating your account via SMS / WhatsApp OTP.
  • Facilitating doorstep pickup and delivery through verified Cleanz24 riders.
  • Sending live order tracking status notifications.

3. User Rights & Account Deletion

In full compliance with Google Play Store & Apple App Store policies, you have complete control over your data. You can permanently delete your Cleanz24 account and all associated personal data directly within the app by going to Profile Screen → Delete Account & Personal Data, or by contacting our team at support@cleanz24.com.

4. Security

We use 256-bit SSL encryption and strict database access controls to safeguard your data. We never sell or rent your personal information.

5. Contact Us

For any privacy inquiries, reach us at support@cleanz24.com.
